Ingredient:

  • Pasta
  • Onion
  • Boiled water
  • Tomato sauce
  • Sugar
  • Ground beef

How to cook it:

Place the past in the boiled water for about 15 min. Salted the water and keep it

In a pan put oil and chopped onion and keep it until onion be gold.

Remove it the onion for the oil, and put the ground beef until it tender. Remove it from the oil.

Put sugar in the same pan and keep the same oil of onion and ground beef. Until caramelized. then Put the tomato sauce.

Steer, Salted and put the spices and but the ground beef and the onion then put the past.
